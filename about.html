About me

Hi, I'm Isabella (Bella) Cimino, a rising junior at the University of Michigan's Stamps School of Art & Design, where I'm pursuing a BA in Communication Design with minors in Business and UX Design, expected to graduate in May 2028. Whatever the project, from developing visual identity and content strategy at CompIntelligence, to creating brand campaigns with Michigan Advertising and Marketing, to founding Creativity That Cares, a 70+ member nonprofit connecting art and community service back home, I keep coming back to the same idea: using creativity as a way to bring people together, whether that's around a brand, a cause, or just a shared moment. That's what draws me to creative marketing, and I'm looking for a summer 2027 internship where I can keep building that skill on a bigger stage.

Beyond the resume, beauty (in both the literal and figurative sense) sits close to how I see the world. I care about the confidence and connection it creates, and I try to bring that same care into small, offbeat things too: last summer at my gallery internship I was gifted a gold-leafed coyote bone, and by the end of the season I'd turned it into a necklace I still wear. When I'm not working on a project, you'll usually find me gardening, running, golfing, or on the pickleball court. But most of all, I'm family-oriented. My siblings and I are constantly finding reasons to see each other, whether it's running a race together, catching a concert, or just hanging out, and that sense of togetherness is what grounds everything else I do.
